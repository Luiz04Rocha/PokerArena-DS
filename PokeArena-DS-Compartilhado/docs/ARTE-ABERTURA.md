# Ilustração de abertura

Na v0.26, a abertura ganhou a praça em pixel art de `ds/assets/title-route.png`, criada por `ds/tools/build_title_route.py` na resolução de 256 × 192. As áreas livres correspondem aos trajetos dos quatro Pokémon. O script `ds/tools/build_title.py` prepara a praça e a logo para a ROM.

Na v0.39, a praça ganhou um piso elevado com fileiras em perspectiva, uma ponte de madeira com corrimãos e uma nascente sobre uma plataforma de pedra. Copas, sombras frias e as quatro rotas em profundidades diferentes acompanham a câmera inclinada. A espuma e os reflexos da água são animados na ROM.

A floresta das versões anteriores foi criada com o gerador de imagens integrado, usando a captura enviada por Luiz como referência de composição. O original está em `ds/assets/title-forest.png`. A partir da v0.24, o mesmo gerador foi usado para recortar a logo enviada por Luiz. O recorte transparente está em `ds/assets/title-logo.png`.

## Prompt da floresta

Use case: illustration-story. Create a single full-bleed 4:3 landscape pixel-art title-screen background for the native Nintendo DSi fangame PokeArena DS. Use the most recent attached screenshot only as the visual composition and art-direction reference for its UPPER forest screen. Render only the forest illustration, no text of any kind, no logo, no UI, no borders, no device, no buttons. Richly shaded hand-crafted pixel-art forest from a late 2000s Nintendo DS monster RPG, with large gnarled trees framing both edges and upper corners, emerald foliage, mossy stones, a little waterfall and blue creek at bottom left, golden trail winding up from the bottom center, distant misty green mountains and blue sky, tiny white and yellow flowers. In the lower right quadrant, a recognizable Leafeon Pokémon, leafy ears and tail, cream fur, full body visible, confident pose facing left, prominently silhouetted and large enough to read at 256x192. Keep the upper left and upper center from x 8% to 70% and y 12% to 58% calm and mostly dark green with simple shadowed forms, as we will overlay our own large gold title there. Keep bottom y 85%-100% relatively dark for legible prompt. Strong silhouette, stepped pixel clusters with warm edge light, moderate palette; avoid painterly blur. Wide 4:3 composition, must read clearly scaled down to 256x192.

## Prompt do recorte da logo

Image edit, background extraction only. The single supplied image is the edit target. Extract the exact existing central Poke ARENA DS emblem as a transparent PNG, preserving its lettering, layout, typography, colors and every part of its existing silhouette. Keep the yellow/blue Poke, stone ARENA, golden DS, both red Poké Balls, the wooden plaque, and all foreground green leaves attached to the logo, including those below DS. Erase absolutely everything from the original environment behind the emblem: sky, clouds, trees, mountains, waterfall, trail and the partially visible Leafeon on the right. Preserve the original lettering and shapes as faithfully as possible; do not redraw, restyle, add a border, add text, or crop off the outer leaves. The result should contain exactly one centered intact logo and true transparent alpha everywhere outside it, with modest transparent padding.
